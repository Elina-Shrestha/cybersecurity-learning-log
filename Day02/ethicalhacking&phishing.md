# Ethical hacking

Breaking into someone's system with permission to find holes before the criminal does. THe factors that should mandatorily be focused are:
- Permission: Always written & authorized
- Scope: Only touch what you're allowed to touch
- Report: find it, report it and fix it

# Stages of attack

- Recon: get intel
- Scanning: map the target(open ports, live hosts)
- Gaining access: exploit the weak link
- Maintain access: plant a backdoor to kep control over time
- COvering tracks: remove logs and evidence to avoid detection

# Tools to use
- Nmap: scans network, whats alive, which port/services is active
- Wireshark: captures and reads network traffic packet by packet
- Burpsuite: intercepts and attacks web apps
- Kali Linux: OS built for hacking, with hundreds of tools preloaded
- Metasploit: framework to launch and manage real exploits
- Social engineering toolkit: builds phishing pages and campaigns

# Social Engineering

Hacking the person not the system. Manipulating the person into giving their credentials using trust, fear, urgency or curiosity. The types of social engineering:
- Phishing- Fake emails/websites that steal data
- Smishing- same trick over sms/text message
- Vishing- fake voice calls
- Pretexting- inventing a believable story to extract info
- Baiting- attaching something tempting(prizes)

# Anatomy of phishing attacks

- Bait: Attacker sends a message posing as someone you trust
- Hook: Creates a sense of urgency/fear
- Fake page: leads you to a fake page that might look similar to an authentic page
- Capture: you enter your credentials in the face page, straight into the attacker's hands
- Exploit: they log in as you and steals everything

# How to spot phishing attacks?
- Urgency
- Fear
- mismatched sender
- spelling errors
- generic greetings
- suspicious links
- ASKS FOR CREDENTIALS

# Prevention

- Turn on MFA
- Never use same password everywhere
- Verify sender
- Hover before click
- Go direct, dont click
- Report if detected
