# Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting system, network or data from digital attacks keeping the information available for the right person and out of reach for the wrong ones.

# The CIA Triad

- ** Confidentiality** : Only authorized person can see the data.
  Affected by: data breach, eavesdropping | Solution: encryption, access control
- ** Integrity **: Data is accurate and has not been tampered with.
  Affected by: malware, unauthorized edits | Solution: hashing, signatures
- ** Availability **: Data ia available anytime.
  Affected by: ransomware, DDoS | Solution: DDoS protection, redundancy

# AAA and DAD

Authentication- proves who you are (password, OTP, signatures)
Authorization- what you're allowed to touch
Accounting- logging what was done by whom

Disclosure- data revealed to wrong eyes
Alteration- data changed quietly
Destruction- data/service knocked offline

# Hackers

People who breaks things to understand them.
- White hat- Tests systems with permission and reports what was found.
- Black hat- Neither has permission nor reports what was found.
- Grey hat- Tests without permission but reports what was found. Still illegal.
- Script kiddie- random test without understanding them. low skill, real damage.
- Hacktivist- attacks to make a political point
- State-sponsored- Government-funded teams

# Types of cyber threats

- Malware- malicious software- viruses, worms, trojan, spyware
- Ransomware- encrypts your files and asks for money
  Steps: Email-> Install-> Spread-> Lock-> Fallout
- Phishing- tricks human into handing over credentials
  How to spot phishing attack?
  Urgency, look-alike domain, spelling errors, generic greeting, hidden links
- Insider threats- trusted employee(malicious/careless)
- Social engineering- hacking the person not the computer
- APTs- Advanced Persistent Threats, funded groups that stay hidden for months

# Attacker's mindset
1. Reckon- Find the target and gather info.
2. Initial access- Get a foothold.
3. Escalate- Go from normal user to administrator
4. Move- Spread across network.
5. Act- Steal data,deploy ransomware or stay back in silence.
